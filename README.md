This repository describes the codes used in Kida et al. 2026 BioRxiv. doi: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.13.765879

Most computational analyses were performed on the NIG supercomputer at the Research Organization of Information and Systems (ROIS) National Institute of Genetics (NIG). These scripts are provided as-is; therefore, you may need to modify them for your local environment.
